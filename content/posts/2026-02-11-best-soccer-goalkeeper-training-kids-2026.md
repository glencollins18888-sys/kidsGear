## Buying Guide

Goalkeeper gloves should fit snugly with about half an inch of extra space at the fingertips. Too tight and they restrict movement; too loose and they affect grip and confidence. Most youth gloves come in sizes 3-7, with size charts based on hand circumference. Measure and check the chart before ordering.

Invest in glove care. Latex palms are grippy but delicate. Wash gloves after every session with lukewarm water, let them air dry (never machine dry), and store them with the palms facing each other. Proper care doubles the lifespan of a pair of gloves.

Reaction training is the highest-value investment for young keepers. Shot-stopping is largely about positioning and reflexes. A reaction ball costs under $10 and provides more reflex development than any other single piece of equipment.

Consider your child's specific weaknesses. If they're afraid of the ball, focus on soft balls and low-impact catching drills. If positioning is the issue, use cones to mark the goal and practice angles. If distribution needs work, practice throwing and kicking drills separately.
