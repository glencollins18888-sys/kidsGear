## Buying Guide

The most important factor when buying basketball equipment for kids is getting the right size. Basketballs come in three main sizes: Size 5 (27.5 inches) for ages 5-8, Size 6 (28.5 inches) for ages 9-12, and Size 7 (29.5 inches, full size) for ages 13 and up. Using a ball that's too large or heavy will develop bad habits.

For hoops, adjustable height is a must. Starting too high discourages young players and teaches them to throw the ball rather than shoot with proper form. Begin at a height where your child can shoot comfortably and raise it as they grow.

When it comes to training aids, focus on fundamentals first. Ball-handling and shooting are the two most important skills, so invest in equipment that develops those areas before moving on to specialized training tools.

Durability matters, especially for outdoor equipment. Kids are tough on gear, and outdoor courts are rough on balls and hoops. Look for products designed specifically for outdoor use if that's where your child will primarily practice.
