## Introduction

Offense gets the attention, but defense wins championships — and that's true at every level of baseball. A child who can field ground balls cleanly, catch fly balls confidently, and make accurate throws is always in the lineup. Fielding is a skill that rewards practice, and the right equipment makes that practice more effective.

The beauty of fielding training is its simplicity. A flat glove, some ground balls, and a wall are enough to build solid fundamentals. These products take it a step further, creating realistic game situations your child can practice at home.

## Buying Guide

Start with grounders before fly balls. Ground ball fielding is the most common defensive play and the foundation of all fielding skills. A player who fields grounders cleanly can play any infield position. Work on proper positioning — glove down, butt low, eyes on the ball.

Soft or reduced-impact balls are essential for young fielders. Fear of the ball is the number one barrier to defensive development. Using safety balls, tennis balls, or small training balls removes the fear factor and lets kids focus on technique. Graduate to real baseballs only when catching mechanics are solid.

Create a routine that mirrors game situations. Don't just hit random ground balls. Call out game scenarios: "Runner on first, ball hit to you at shortstop — where's the throw?" This trains decision-making alongside physical skills.

Wall drills are free and incredibly effective. Throwing a tennis ball against a concrete wall and fielding the rebound develops quick hands, reactions, and proper fielding posture. Do this for 10 minutes daily and watch defensive skills skyrocket.
