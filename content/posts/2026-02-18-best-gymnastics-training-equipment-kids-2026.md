## Buying Guide

Safety must be the top consideration for home gymnastics equipment. Always place mats under and around any elevated equipment like bars and beams. Never let children attempt skills at home that they haven't first learned with a coach. Establish clear rules about what skills are appropriate for home practice.

Ceiling height matters more than you might think. Before buying a bar or practicing handstands, measure your ceiling height and ensure there's adequate clearance. A minimum of 8-foot ceilings is recommended for most home gymnastics activities.

Start with a mat. Even before buying any other equipment, a quality folding mat gives kids a safe surface for stretching, handstands, cartwheels, and basic tumbling. It's the most versatile piece of equipment you can own.

Quality and weight limits are critical. Always check the weight capacity of bars and beams, and choose products designed specifically for gymnastics rather than general-purpose play equipment. Gymnastics equipment needs to handle dynamic forces, not just static weight.
